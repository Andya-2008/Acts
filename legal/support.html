Common questions

Sign in

Use the email and password you registered with. If you forgot your password, use “Forgot password?” on the sign-in screen.

Friends

Add friends by username, invite link, or contacts (optional). You control who sees your feed in Settings → Privacy.

Report or block

On a deed post, use the menu to report content or block a user. Blocked users are hidden from your feed and friend list.

Delete account

Settings → Account → Delete account. This permanently removes your Acts profile and data we store for your account. Can’t open the app? See Delete your account.
