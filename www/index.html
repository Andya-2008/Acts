Deeds from the Acts catalog

Small enough to start today.

Acts rotates approachable prompts across various categories like community, emotional, environmental, creative, adventure, general, and self-care. Some take a minute. Some ask you to make a memory. All of them nudge kindness, meaning, and connection into your life.

Community

Hold the door open for someone.

A tiny gesture that makes someone feel seen, especially when their hands are full.

Emotional

Reach out to someone who looks down or lonely.

A kind message, a short chat, or simply sitting with someone can matter more than it looks.

Environmental

Pick up a few pieces of litter you see on the ground.

Care for your neighborhood in a way people can notice and follow.

Self-care

Write down three things that went well today.

Acts can be for you, too. Noticing the good on purpose makes more of it stand out.
