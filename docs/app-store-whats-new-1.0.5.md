# App Store — What's New (1.0.5)

Paste into App Store Connect → version **1.0.5** → **What's New in This Version** (English U.S.).

Apple allows up to **4,000 characters**; the copy below is well under that.

---

## Recommended (paste as-is)

```
Acts 1.0.5 is about getting started, staying connected, and moving through the app with less friction.

NEW & IMPROVED
• Invite friends from the app and earn seeds when someone joins from your link
• Friendlier empty states when your circle is still small — with a clear path to grow it
• First-act spotlight on Tasks so new members know exactly where to begin
• Tap notifications (or items in your inbox) to jump to the right place — a deed, Friends, or activity
• Seasonal challenges: add an optional photo when you log what you did
• Sign in with your username or phone, not just email
• Dark themes and custom deed cards are easier to read

Thank you for showing up for others. One act at a time.
```
