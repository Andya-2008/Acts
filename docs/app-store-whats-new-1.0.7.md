# App Store — What's New (1.0.7)

Paste into App Store Connect → version **1.0.7** → **What's New in This Version** (English U.S.).

Apple allows up to **4,000 characters**; the copy below is well under that.

---

## Recommended (paste as-is)

```
Acts 1.0.7 adds widgets, stronger account security, and polish across the app.

NEW & IMPROVED
• Verify your mobile number by text message when you sign up or sign in with Google or Apple
• Home screen widgets on iPhone and Android — see your streak and suggested acts at a glance
• Kindness Arcade is now called Rewards — same seeds and perks, clearer name
• Crop your profile photo when you pick one from the camera or library
• Gentle reminder when a newer version of Acts is available on the App Store

Thank you for showing up for others. One act at a time.
```
